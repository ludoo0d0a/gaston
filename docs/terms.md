## 1. Description of Service
Gaston is a mobile application that aggregates data from various third-party sources to provide information about fuel stations, electric vehicle charging points, and other points of interest.

Optionally, Gaston may offer a **driving assistant** (assistant d’aide à la conduite) that announces **danger zones** and posted speed limits (VMA). This is **not** a radar/enforcement-control locator: it does not display the precise position of police or automated controls.
